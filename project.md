## Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

> The use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools is permitted for the NASA Space Apps Challenge and can be used as a tool to accelerate innovation and creativity. However, generated content may not use, contain, or modify any NASA branding or design elements (such as the NASA logos, flags, or mission identifiers). You must also clearly indicate where and how AI tools were used in your project, including:
>
> - **Images and videos:** Must include a visible watermark indicating that they are AI-generated.
> - **Audio:** Must acknowledge AI generation in descriptive text and metadata.
